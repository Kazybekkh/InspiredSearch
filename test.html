Test Page for Real AI Research Paper Explorer

This tests the 3-level hierarchical exploration with real AI research papers and clickable titles.

Real AI Research Papers:

  • Root: "Attention Is All You Need" (The foundational Transformer paper)
  • Level 1: BERT, Vision Transformer, GPT, Switch Transformer
  • Level 2: RoBERTa, ALBERT, DeiT, Swin Transformer, GPT-3, PaLM, GLaM
  • All papers: Link to actual ArXiv URLs and research papers

New Clickable Title Feature:

  • Click paper titles: Opens the actual research paper in a new tab
  • Click elsewhere on box: Selects/navigates to that paper (collapse behavior)
  • Visual feedback: Titles show blue hover effects and underlines
  • Real URLs: Direct links to ArXiv papers and OpenAI research

Expected Features:

  • Red toggle button in the bottom right corner
  • Main paper with "Find New Papers" (white button) - draggable
  • "Find More" buttons (blue) on Level 1 papers
  • Horizontal drag handles (⋮⋮) on all sub-boxes - drag left/right only
  • 3 hover colors: Green (Level 1), Orange (Level 2), Green (back button)
  • Improved line alignment - connectors properly aligned to box centers
  • Complete collapse when any paper is selected
  • "← Go Back" button (appears when navigated)

New Drag Features:

  • Main paper: Full drag (move anywhere)
  • Level 1 & 2 papers: Horizontal drag only (±300px limit - increased!)
  • Drag handles: Small ⋮⋮ icons in top-left of each sub-box
  • Live connector updates: Lines update in real-time during drag
  • Extended container: 1800px width (doubled) prevents cutoff issues

Test Sequence:

  1. Click red toggle → see "Attention Is All You Need" paper
  2. Click the paper title → opens ArXiv page in new tab
  3. Drag main paper around → test full movement
  4. Click "Find New Papers" → see BERT and Vision Transformer papers
  5. Click BERT title → opens BERT paper on ArXiv
  6. Click elsewhere on BERT box → navigates to BERT as root paper
  7. Click "Find More" on any Level 1 paper → see Level 2 papers
  8. Click any Level 2 title → opens that paper's ArXiv page
  9. Click "← Go Back" → return to previous paper
